# Domain Framework: Executive Reporting & Goal Tracking

## 1. Data Ingestion & Normalization
1. **Fetch Goal Data:** Retrieve current status, progress, and blockers from Jira (epics, boards, tickets) and Google Sheets (health metrics).
2. **Normalize Metrics:** Translate technical metrics (e.g., tickets, SLAs, points) into business outcomes (e.g., efficiency, customer support health).
3. **Assess Confidence:** Assign a confidence score (e.g., RAG status or 0-100%) to each goal based on current progress and identified blockers.

## 2. Impediment & Risk Analysis
1. **Identify Blockers:** Extract blockers from Jira and cross-reference with goal progress.
2. **Apply "Five Whats":** For each major blocker, determine: Goal, Blocker, Impact, Tried Solutions, Ideal Fix.
3. **Determine Escalation:** Decide if the blocker requires leadership intervention or can be resolved within the team.

## 3. Report Generation (Leadership Focus)
1. **Executive Summary:** Write a 1-sentence big-picture blurb and 2-4 bullet points with quantified impact.
2. **Goal Status:** Present RAG status for each major goal (Alert Management, Efficiency, Customer Support, Productivity).
3. **Impediments & Decisions:** Highlight blockers requiring leadership action, using the "Five Whats" framework.

## 4. Report Generation (Team Focus)
1. **Team Narrative:** Summarize the week's focus, shipped items, and current blockers in a collaborative tone.
2. **Transparency:** Explain decisions made and why, connecting daily work to the semester goals.
3. **Recognition:** Highlight specific contributions from team members (Adriel, Antonio, Bruna, Daniel, Wellington, Pedro, Jonas, Maria Eduarda).

## 5. Review & Refinement
1. **Check Clarity:** Ensure the leadership report passes the "30-60 second test" and the team report avoids corporate jargon.
2. **Verify Actionability:** Confirm that every highlighted risk in the leadership report implies a plausible management action.
3. **Final Polish:** Adjust tone for the respective audiences (decisive/strategic for leadership, engaging/transparent for the team).
